English translation: Marmaduke Pickthall, "The Meaning of the Glorious Koran"

Translator: Muhammad Marmaduke Pickthall (born 7 April 1875, London; died 19 May 1936,
St Ives). First published 1930 (London: Alfred A. Knopf / George Allen & Unwin; New York:
Alfred A. Knopf).

Status: public domain.
- Germany and the EU: the term ended 70 years after the translator's death, on 31 December
  2006 (Section 64 UrhG, Directive 2006/116/EC).
- United States: published in 1930; works of 1930 entered the public domain on 1 January 2026
  (95 years from publication).

Digital text: the verses marked "P:" in the e-text "Three translations of the Koran
(Al-Qur'an) side by side", eBook #16955 of gutenberg.org (released 27 October 2005, updated
12 December 2020), file https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/16955/pg16955.txt, pinned by
SHA-256 in data/sources.toml. Only Pickthall's verses are used; the two other translations in
that file are not. The verses are taken as they are, divided by the standard (Kufan) count of
6,236 verses; only the line breaks within a verse are joined with a space.

This digital text is not a verbatim copy of the 1930 print. Its origin is unknown ("a file
whose origins we don't know", re-proofed against paper copies). Compared with the 1930 print,
"God" is replaced by "Allah" in some verses (e.g. 2:163), "Owner" by "Master" in 1:4, some
punctuation differs, and the verse division follows the standard count instead of the 1930
numbering. Such mechanical changes carry no copyright of their own, so the text is public
domain like the original.
